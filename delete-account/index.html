Delete your Naveto account

Naveto, the German grammar app. Last updated: 6 October 2026

A Naveto profile is optional. If you created one and want it gone, you can delete it yourself in the app, at any time, in a few seconds.

In the app

  1. Open Naveto and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap your profile (the first card, with your name).
  3. Scroll to Delete your account and tap Delete account, then Delete for good.

The deletion happens straight away. You are signed out, and the app keeps working as before, without an account.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email mihir.m.bavisi@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Naveto account". The account is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: email address, name, and sign-in details (including the link to Sign in with Apple or Google, if you used one).
  • The copy of your learning progress saved to your account.
  • The reports and suggestions you sent from the app.

Nothing is kept in a "deactivated" state. The server's own backups, where they exist, are overwritten within 7 days.

What is not deleted

  • The progress on your phone. It stays until you uninstall the app, or reset it on the phone.
  • A backup file you exported yourself, wherever you saved it.
