Privacy Policy — Naveto

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Naveto does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Naveto saves your learning progress so it is still there when you reopen the app. That includes which lessons you have completed, your review schedule, your streak, the mistakes you have made while practising, and the settings you chose during setup.

All of it is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. None of it is uploaded, backed up to our servers, or accessible to us. We have no servers that receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Naveto removes everything the app has stored. There is nothing held anywhere else, so there is nothing further to request or delete. You can also clear the app's data from your device's Android settings without uninstalling.

Permissions

Naveto requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or phone identifiers.

Internet access

The app declares the standard Android internet permission, which is required by the framework Naveto is built with. The app itself makes no network requests during normal use: all lessons and vocabulary are bundled inside the app and work fully offline.

Children

Naveto is intended for adult learners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If Naveto ever adds a feature that does handle your data — for example optional accounts, cloud sync of your progress, or purchases — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be described here with a new date at the top. Continuing to use a version of the app released before a change does not opt you into it.
