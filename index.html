A wrong answer, from a real lesson

It tells you why, not just that.

A wrong answer does not just turn red. Naveto names the mistake (wrong case, wrong gender, verb in the wrong slot), explains that exact error, and brings the sentence back until it sticks.

English stays on the card. The language you chose sits under it, and the German stays German, so you learn the words you will see in Germany. Try it:

der Lehrer
addressed as Sie
Ich trinke kein Kaffee.
I don't drink coffee.
keinen
kein
keine
keinem
Not quite: keinen
kein is the nominative masculine. Kaffee is what you drink, the object of trinken, so it is accusative: keinen.

Built for people moving to Germany, or already there.

Grammar, not phrases

Anyone can memorise a phrase. Each lesson takes one piece of grammar, the accusative, verb position, adjective endings, and moves you from noticing it to producing it alone.

Your mistakes, fixed

Every mistake is kept in your journal and comes back days apart. It counts as fixed after four right answers, not after replaying the lesson.

The exam, in its shape

Ten practice papers in the format of the Goethe and telc exams, parts numbered as they will be, scored part by part, the listening played once when the exam plays it once.

Spoken, and offline

Every German sentence is recorded in six voices, so each character sounds like themselves. The whole course, audio included, is inside the app.

A cast, because who is speaking matters.

German says du to family and friends and Sie to a teacher or a stranger. The characters make that visible: you answer the grandmother differently from the egg. Get it right and they react; get four in a row and there is fire.

Explained in eight languages.

From A1 to B1 every lesson has a note written for speakers of your language: what works the same, and what will trip you up. From B2 up, English carries you. The interface, the lessons and every word's meaning are translated too.

Marathi, Telugu and Malayalam are new. If a sentence reads wrongly, the Report button under every answer sends it straight to us.

Free while Naveto is new.

Every level, every lesson and every practice paper. No ads. You can use all of it without an account; a profile is optional, and only there so your progress can follow you to a new phone.
